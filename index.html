I study what neural networks know, where they keep it, and whether we can check.

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in Computer Science & AI at IIIT Delhi. I work on mechanistic interpretability, representation learning and AI safety: reading what is stored inside a model's layers, testing whether it is actually used, and building simple tools that catch models when they go wrong.

Right now I'm a research scholar at MARS V (Cambridge AI Safety Hub) with Yossi Gandelsman, studying whether LLMs can report on their own computation. Before this I worked with Randall Balestriero (Brown / Meta FAIR) and Anubha Gupta (SBI Lab). I also founded StarkVision, a student research group of 20+ people.

MK·01

Research

One question runs through most of my work: what does a model represent internally, and how much of that does it actually use? I approach it from four directions.

I · INTERPRETABILITY & SAFETY

Looking inside the model

Residual-stream analysis, probing, steering and causal interventions. Can an LLM know things about its own computation? Can we spot a jailbreak or a hallucination from the activations alone?

MARS V · Shiba AI · Whisper RSC (ICML-W '26)
II · REPRESENTATIONS ACROSS DEPTH

The last layer isn't always best

Useful information rises and falls across depth. I measure where it lives, how it's shaped, and how to read it out from intermediate layers.

ICML '26 Spotlight · NeurIPS '26 · UniReps '25
III · SELF-SUPERVISED & WORLD MODELS

Learning without labels

JEPA-style predictive models for science, planning and markets. Contrastive learning, distillation and calibration for foundation models.

Lens-JEPA (GSoC) · DroidPlanner · TabPFN distillation
IV · DETECTING SYNTHETIC CONTENT

Telling real from generated

Lightweight, interpretable detectors for AI-written code, AI-generated music and social-media deepfakes.

EMNLP '26 · TMLR · ICMR-W '25 · NeurIPS-W '25
